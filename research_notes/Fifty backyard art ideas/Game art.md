# What other games can teach this backyard

## How do the best landmarks and locations stay readable?

### Takeaway

Give each part of the yard a recognisable shape, then concentrate small visual jokes around the places where players stop.

### Cited Findings

- Valve designed its two bases with different materials and architectural shapes as well as different colours. It also limited repeated geometry such as bridge trusses and telephone poles to control visual noise. Sections 4.2–4.3. [Valve's Team Fortress 2 art paper](https://cdn.steamstatic.com/apps/valve/2007/NPAR07_IllustrativeRenderingInTeamFortress2.pdf)
- House House designer Nico Disseldorp describes starting with a setting and the interactions a level required. Jake Strasser then sourced arrangements from real locations, combining features from several places into one playable neighbourhood. [House House's developer interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/road-to-the-igf-house-house-s-i-untitled-goose-game-i-)
- Splatoon producer Hisashi Nogami describes starting with the game activity, then imagining a plausible place and culture for people who would take part, down to fashion and music. [Nintendo's producer interview](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/News/2016/October/Learn-all-about-Splatoon-s-unique-style-in-our-exclusive-interview--1146288.html)

### Inferences

1. Give the skyline three distinct neighbouring backyards. For example, a scalloped caravan annexe, a high asymmetric carport and a row of sawtooth workshop windows let house shapes double as spoken directions from the playable roof.
2. Distinguish the fixed Heist junctions by silhouette. A single arch of bent irrigation pipe, a stepped stack of slats and a broad shade-cloth triangle can be remembered as “hoop”, “steps” and “sail”. Team doors remain free to move between rounds.
3. Treat the warm side of the picnic table and the cool side of the veranda as separate planes. Darken the underside of the roof while leaving the front-facing wall behind the carrier pale enough for body contrast.
4. Make the tank-to-roof jump legible with two contact marks. Paint on the tank lip is rubbed smooth at takeoff and the galvanised ridge cap is polished where feet land. Both marks correspond to the existing reachable trajectory.
5. Build a sight gag from two houses. A single tennis ball sits high in a gutter. On the opposite side is a scuffed rectangle on the fence where someone practised serves. The high spot is an optional roof discovery.
6. Put a back-door view through one partly open kitchen window. A dish-drying rack and an enamel jug sit under a half-drawn blind. Construct one shallow interior set and show its sunlit edge from the veranda.
7. Fix recurring camera shimmer at its source by comparing a walking shot along diagonal corrugated roofs, wire and Heist walls. Soften distant rib detail and separate overlapping decals where the motion capture exposes the problem.

### Gaps

These sources do not test Marcus's arena. Check junction recognition during a run and the cliff of contrast at the veranda in screenshots from player height. The underlying world stays the same size.

## How can details express an Australian party culture?

### Takeaway

Make the recurring graphic designs look like possessions of the same household, with one reason for each prop to be where it is.

### Cited Findings

- Grounded assistant art director Paul Fish describes brainstorming fictional products and having a teammate invent their logos. Even the packaging symbol carries the in-world manufacturer's attitude. [Interview with Grounded artists](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/grounded-release-concept-art-obsidian/amp)
- House House planned slapstick situations around a place and the people who used it, selecting from a list of gags for each setting. [House House's developer interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/road-to-the-igf-house-house-s-i-untitled-goose-game-i-)

### Inferences

8. Design a house-made snag sauce with a tomato shaped like a footy. Show an intact decal on Dazza's squeeze bottle and a sun-bleached version on the corresponding crate in the shed.
9. Give the family a pegboard of retired backyard trophies. The first cup is a turned timber stump, the second is a spoon mounted upside down, and the latest is an actual cup, all in one small silhouette group on the veranda.
10. Hang a faded fixture poster for a fictional local footy club beside the back door. An old torn corner and two crooked strips of tape establish its history without asking players to read body text.
11. Put an elderly neighbour's silent reaction behind a curtained window. After a nearby miss, the blind briefly lifts, a pair of eyes appears, then disappears. Time it after impact so nothing crosses the line of the throw.
12. Make the gnomes' spectator gag pay off once a round. During the quiet results beat, show one new miniature scarf or pennant on the winner's side. The existing gnomes remain recognisably ornamental during play.

### Gaps

Use original symbols, labels and illustrations. A factual reference establishes a technique, not a licence to reproduce photos, logos or character designs. The Grounded interview's subject is source attribution, not a proposal to change player scale.

## How can characters and items read during a chase?

### Takeaway

Show one visible change in posture or shape that remains legible across a yard. Keep transient effects close to their cause and brief enough to leave the next throw visible.

### Cited Findings

- Mediatonic principal artist Ash Kerins says patterns help identify a bean in a crowd and recommends zooming out and hiding half a costume to test if its design still reads. [Fall Guys character-design notes](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=224711)
- Valve used silhouette-only checks in concept work, plus value and hue changes and rim highlights to identify characters in varied lighting. [Valve's Team Fortress 2 art paper](https://cdn.steamstatic.com/apps/valve/2007/NPAR07_IllustrativeRenderingInTeamFortress2.pdf)
- House House designed the goose's verbs to make its body feel expressive and performative, and describes how taking objects worked as a physical comedy interaction. [House House's developer interview](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/game-platforms/road-to-the-igf-house-house-s-i-untitled-goose-game-i-)

### Inferences

13. Add broad existing-singlet patterns such as a diagonal sash, two hoops or a shoulder block. Preserve the team-colour area and the printed back name. Check the torso with the head occluded from across the yard.
14. Put a floppy ear and a contrasting stitched muzzle on the carried teddy. The ear lags on a turn and settles against the carrier. Players can read the objective in side view while both hands are busy.
15. Give a clean catch a one-beat recoil proportional to the item. A heavy object pushes elbows inward. A noodle bends over one shoulder before straightening. Do it after the catch resolves so timing and controls stay familiar.
16. Give recovering bots a brief purposeful gesture tied to the existing state sequence. Brush a grass blade off the singlet as control returns. An airborne bot can pin its thongs back against its soles and let them settle on landing.
17. Rank environmental contrast along a real throw. Foliage and fence stay quiet behind a side-on throw, while a narrow edge tint on the teddy separates it against both sky and wall. Test an item against prop shade at hand size, cross-yard size and browser resolution.
18. Give the banked-teddy event one local, diegetic reaction. The bank's mechanical number flap rotates once when the existing bank event lands. Keep the animation inside the base instead of putting confetti between opponents and the camera.

### Gaps

These are proposed presentation changes. No source supplies appropriate animation times, shader budgets or contact-mark positions for the current game. Keep rules and collision unchanged for these examples, and visually check them in both builds before approving.
