# Smoko shelter surroundings

Editable source: `smoko-surroundings.blend`. Rebuild in Blender by executing `build_surroundings.py`; it creates a separate scene and exports only selected surroundings to `rust/crates/bbq_app/assets/models/smoko_surroundings.glb`.

`surroundings-layout.json` supplies sandstone centres and width/depth/height in game metres. Blender uses x, -game-z, height; glTF converts to game Y-up. `bush_servo::LANDSCAPE_SOLIDS` must match the JSON. Bounds are verified before export in `surroundings-bounds.json`. Keep this table synchronized when editing solid shapes. Four broad cardinal lanes and the delivery circle are protected by core tests. Grass and saltbush are decorative, with no collision.

Tuning: seed and lane half-width are in the layout. Grass counts, heights, shrub placements and palette are in the authoring script. Four embedded 128px stone-grain textures are generated by make_sandstone.py. No transparency, animation or external asset dependencies. Nine material batches and 4,978 triangles.

This is a first stylised landscape pass, not a claim to match the concept's photoreal detail. Larger trees, distant scenery, servo detail and small furniture props are separate stages.
