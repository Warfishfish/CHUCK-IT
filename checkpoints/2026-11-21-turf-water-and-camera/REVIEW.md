# Teddy Heist turf, Backyard water and character motion

21 November 2026 · prototype 0.23.48

- **Heist turf.** Regenerated the shared tile in Blender with brighter midtones and less fine grain. The floor reads more clearly in shade while retaining the contrast at wall shadows. The 2-, 3- and 4-flag layouts were checked at walking height in the desktop and Chrome WebGPU builds. - **Backyard water.** Sprinkler drops travel and fall independently after leaving the sweeping nozzle. Pool entry starts three flat, staggered rings that expand and fade, including on Low. A balloon sprays a higher crown of shorter-lived water, and varying body drips and glints make the soaked target readable while it runs. The hit and four-second wet slowdown were checked in the native game. The legacy browser ran a controlled check of sprinkler travel and the pool ripple timing. [Water capture](backyard-water.jpg) · [Sprinkler and balloon in motion](backyard-water-motion.webp)
- **Feet and carried view.** The gait timing matches stance travel more closely and resumes with both feet on the ground after a jump. The carried camera checks obstruction after position smoothing and again while blending back to first person. A native host used the game's standard grab and release functions in a staged two-player round. The WebGPU guest saw the two characters while being carried around the red base wall, remained in the external view during the throw and returned to first person.
