# Teddy Heist player throws and outer halls

Press F to pick up a downed player. Hold F and release to throw. A tap puts them down. Heist uses the host's carry and flight state for both local and online input. Controlled tests measured 5.32 m from the carrier in Rust and 5.45 m in the legacy browser on each clear test lane. Walls can shorten a throw. A separate Rust test delivers the guest's F presses and release to the host and checks that the host launches in the guest's facing direction.

All four perimeter halls have pickup slots. Ten are stocked at the whistle and the others can refill during play. Painted bays, four hall colours, stripe counts and ribbed service panels identify the lanes. [Legacy browser supply bay](supplies.png)

Bots keep waypoints longer, slow down before close turns, replan when progress stalls and prefer different nearby raid targets where several exist. Automated 20-participant runs still recorded steals and banks on each layout. The recorded bank totals were 8, 12 and 16. These checks exercise movement and objectives. A full human match is still needed to judge how the pacing feels.

Online Heist uses protocol 32. Both players need the updated build for host-controlled player throws.
