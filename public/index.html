        What's on the lawn

        The bar

        VP +18 Wine +26 Rum shot +34

        Walk up to the bar by the back fence and press R. Drinking fills your drunk meter, which drops again as you sober up. Once you're tipsy the yard starts to wobble, your view sways and you drift off course when you walk. Everyone else can see it too: drunk players sway, stagger and flail. It all gets worse the more you drink. Get properly drunk and you'll stack it now and then: you're down for 20 seconds unless a mate holds R next to you to help you up (+25 for them). You're slower and can't throw while you drink, and a hit makes you spill it.

        The BBQ

        Dazza is cooking by the back fence. Nick a raw steak or fish off his table (R) and slap someone with it: they're stunned for 3 seconds and you get +50. Dazza yells and swings his spatula at thieves, and if you keep stealing he'll chase you round the yard.
